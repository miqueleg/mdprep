# Metal sites

`syrb2_site_docked_substrate.pdb` is the SyrB2 iron site of
`../input_hydrogens/syrb2_site.pdb` (PDB 2FCT, chain A) with the water on the
iron, HOH 1072, replaced by a small "docked substrate": ethanol, residue SUB
950, its oxygen where the water's was (2.24 A from the iron) and its carbons
pointing into the pocket, at least 3.3 A from every other atom. It stands for
a substrate docked too close to an iron with no water of its own, which mdprep
proposes as one of the iron's bonds and the user has to be able to untick.
